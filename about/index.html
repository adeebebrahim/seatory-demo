What we offer

  • Sofas & Lounge Chairs

    Swivel lounge chairs and sofas for living rooms, reception areas and café lounges. Sit in them at our Mangalore showroom before you decide.

  • Beds & Bedroom

    King and queen size beds, with and without storage, in wood and upholstered finishes. Customers from Mangalore and Udupi can view the range at our showroom.

  • Dining

    Dining sets for every home, from four-seater tables for smaller flats to six-seater family sets. Compare tops, chairs and finishes at our Mangalore showroom.

  • Office Furniture

    Fully imported mesh office chairs from mid-back to high-back, plus workstations for teams. Tell us your team size on WhatsApp and we will suggest options.

  • Café, Dining & Bar Chairs

    Leatherette, cane and upholstered chairs for dining rooms, cafés and restaurants, plus swivel bar chairs for counters and home bars.

  • Storage

    Wardrobes and storage units that keep homes and offices organised. Ask us about sizes and finishes that suit your room.
